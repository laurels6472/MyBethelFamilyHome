BETHEL FAMILY ALERTS — SETUP (about 15 minutes, free, no credit card)
=====================================================================
You do Part A and B ONE time. Part C is done once on each phone.

PART A — Make the free alert helper (on your computer or phone browser)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Go to:  dash.cloudflare.com/sign-up
   Type your email and a password. Tap Sign up.
   Open the email Cloudflare sends you and tap the verify link.
   (If it asks for a plan, choose FREE. It never asks for a card.)

2. After you sign in, look at the menu on the left.
   Tap  "Workers & Pages"  (it may be under "Compute").
   Tap the blue  "Create"  button.
   Tap  "Create Worker"  (or "Start with Hello World!").

3. In the name box, type:  bethel-alerts
   Tap  "Deploy".

4. When it says it is deployed, tap  "Edit code".
   A code box opens. Click inside it, press Select All
   (Ctrl+A on a computer, or touch-and-hold then "Select all" on a phone),
   and press Delete so the box is EMPTY.

5. Open the file I gave you named  bethel-alerts-worker.js
   Select ALL of it, Copy it, then Paste it into the empty code box.
   Tap  "Deploy"  (top right).

6. Look for the address of your helper. It looks like:
       https://bethel-alerts.YOURNAME.workers.dev
   Copy that whole address.
   (Test: open it in a new tab. You should see:  Bethel alert relay is running. ✅ )

   Keep bethel-alerts-worker.js private. Do not post it anywhere.

PART B — Tell the app the address (Laurel, one time)
----------------------------------------------------
7. Upload the new app files to GitHub like always (v114).
8. Open the app. Tap Menu ▸ Settings ▸ "Alerts & sound on this device".
   Find the box  "RELAY ADDRESS".
   Paste the address from step 6. Tap  Save.

PART C — Turn alerts on, on EACH phone (parents and kids)
---------------------------------------------------------
9. iPHONE / iPAD only: first open the app in Safari, tap the Share button,
   tap "Add to Home Screen", and then ALWAYS open the app from that new icon.
   ANDROID: tap the 3 dots ▸ "Install app" (or "Add to Home screen").

10. In the app: Menu ▸ Settings ▸ "Alerts & sound on this device".
    Tap  "Turn on closed-app alerts".  When the phone asks, tap  ALLOW.

11. Tap  "Send me a test".  Then close the app (swipe it away) or lock the screen.
    In a few seconds the phone should light up and make its alert sound.

IF SOMETHING DOESN'T WORK
-------------------------
- "Not allowed / blocked": open the phone's Settings ▸ Notifications ▸ find the app ▸ turn Allow ON.
- Nothing arrives on iPhone: make sure you opened the app from the Home Screen icon (not Safari).
- Wrong address: in step 8 the address must start with https:// and end with .workers.dev
- Still stuck: tell me exactly which step number and what you see on the screen.

WHO GETS ALERTS
---------------
The same rules as before: parents get clock-ins and requests that need approval,
kids get their own messages and approvals. A phone that has these alerts on will
not also get the old ntfy alerts, so there are no doubles.
